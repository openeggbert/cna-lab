# Original identity and legal hygiene

Iron Shadows is an original project and must not be marketed as a remake, unofficial sequel, replacement, port, or recreation of Mafia or any other existing game.

Permitted high-level genre ideas include a historical city, automobiles, crime drama, story missions, interiors, police response, dialogue, and cinematics. Do not copy protected expression such as specific characters, names, dialogue, plot sequence, mission structure, map layout, landmark arrangement, UI, logos, music, sound recordings, vehicle branding, textures, or models.

`Iron Shadows`, `Iron City`, the sample characters, and the prototype delivery mission are working original placeholders. A professional trademark search is still required before public commercial branding.

## Naming status

The earlier internal candidate `Iron Lantern` was rejected after a preliminary collision check. `Iron Shadows` is only a better working candidate, not a cleared commercial mark. Keep the display name, executable slug, CMake identifier, namespace, save-format identifier, and asset metadata synchronized when renaming. See `docs/renaming.md`.
